Use rules

Do

  • Use the supplied file, unaltered.
  • Give the mark at least the cap-height of the K as clear space on all sides.
  • Minimum digital size 28 px. Minimum print size 8 mm.
  • Monochrome for digital. Heritage colour only for packaging & print.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or add shadows / glows / effects.
  • Recolour the mark, or swap in a new typeface.
  • Place low-contrast on a background where the mark disappears.
  • Re-typeset or vector-trace the mark.
